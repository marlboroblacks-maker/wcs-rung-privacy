WCS Rung Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

WCS Rung is an offline CNC, controls, and engineering learning app for iPhone and iPad. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Information in the app

WCS Rung saves lesson progress, selected lessons, example programs, calculator entries, and other training settings on your device so you can continue where you left off. The app does not require an account. WCS Engineering does not receive this information from the app, and the app does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

External reference links

Some lessons link to external manufacturer, standards, and educational reference pages. Opening one leaves WCS Rung and uses your browser. Those sites may handle information under their own privacy policies. WCS Rung does not send your saved training work to those sites.

Retention and removal

Your training data remains on your device unless you change it or remove the app. Device backups may include app data according to your device and backup settings. Removing WCS Rung deletes its local app data from the device, subject to any separate device backups.

Changes

If WCS Rung's features or data practices change, this page will be updated with a new revision date.
